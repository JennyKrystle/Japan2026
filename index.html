Shopping — What & Where

Everything is 5–15 min walk from Solaria. Shops open 10 AM. Show your passport at checkout for ~8% tax refund when you spend ¥5,000+ per store.

⚠️ You're arriving without many clothes — Day 1 Saturday is the day to sort this

Mina Tenjin opens at 10 AM — go straight there after breakfast. Uniqlo and GU are in the same building. Get outfits for the whole 7 days. Budget around ¥15,000–25,000 for both of you combined.

🏨
Solaria Stage + Solaria Plaza · Inside and Next to Your Hotel
You don't even need to go outside. Solaria Stage is directly attached to the hotel — fashion, beauty, pharmacy (OHGA), and a supermarket open 7 AM–11 PM for snacks, toiletries, and anything you forgot. Solaria Plaza is right next door with more shops and connects directly to the Tenjin underground mall. Both open 10 AM–8:30 PM daily.
👗
Mina Tenjin · Your Main Shopping Stop · 8 min walk from hotel
Start here on Day 1. Kyushu's largest Uniqlo (W 1F · M 2F) + the only full-lineup GU in all of Kyushu (2F) in one building. For her: GU lace tulle dress, GU floral shirring camisole dress, Uniqlo linen camisole dress — all summer 2025 pieces, genuinely pretty and photogenic. For him: Uniqlo linen shorts + AIRism polo. Loft, Theory, and Matsumoto Kiyoshi drugstore also inside.
📍 Mina Tenjin (Start Here)Website
🏬
Iwataya + Mitsukoshi Department Stores
Luxury brands (Gucci, Prada, Chanel, Louis Vuitton) on upper floors + the best depachika food basements in Fukuoka. Mitsukoshi basement is your pasalubong headquarters — beautifully packaged Japanese sweets, Fukuoka-exclusive snacks, Hakata mentaiko, all gift-wrapped. Tax-free counter on each floor.
📍 Iwataya 📍 Mitsukoshi
🛒
Tenjin Chikagai · Underground Mall · Directly Connected to Your Hotel
150+ shops underground below all of Tenjin with European-style arched ceilings. Your go-to when it rains — connects Solaria Stage, Mina Tenjin, Daimaru, and Mitsukoshi without ever going outside. Accessories, fashion, snacks, souvenirs. Completely air-conditioned.
Website
🕶️
Daimyo · Boutiques, Vintage, Independent Stores
A completely different energy from the malls — independent Japanese boutiques, local streetwear, vintage denim, Fukuoka-made goods. Best explored on Day 3 afternoon during the Tenjin walk, specifically during the Daimyo wander stop.
💊
Matsumoto Kiyoshi · Drugstore · Multiple Tenjin Locations
The best Japanese skincare, sunscreen, face masks, vitamins, and collagen. Japanese sunscreen (Anessa, Biore, Skin Aqua) is genuinely exceptional quality and significantly cheaper than in Manila. Tax-free over ¥5,000.
🎒
Canal City Hakata · Worth a Stop on Day 2
Already in this area when you visit FUK Coffee + Kushida Shrine. Has the Ghibli store (Donguri Republic), Sanrio, Muji, and Ramen Stadium. Note: Yufuin-exclusive Ghibli items are only available at the Yufuin shop — not here.

💴 Cash — Always Have Some

Yatai stalls are cash only. Small shrines and some Yufuin snack stalls too. Keep ¥15,000–20,000 cash on you at all times. ATMs at 7-Eleven accept foreign cards.

🌡️ June Weather — It Is Hot

27–32°C, humid, rainy season. Bring a compact umbrella — it will rain at least once. The underground mall connects most of Tenjin without going outside. Light fabrics only.

📱 Internet — Get This Before You Fly
