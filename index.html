Everything is 5–15 min walk from Solaria. Shops open 10 AM. Show your passport at checkout for ~8% tax refund when you spend ¥5,000+ per store.

⚠️ You're arriving without many clothes — Day 1 Saturday is the day to sort this

Mina Tenjin opens at 10 AM — go straight there after breakfast. Uniqlo and GU are in the same building. Get outfits for the whole 7 days. Budget around ¥15,000–25,000 for both of you combined.

You don't even need to go outside. Solaria Stage is directly attached to the hotel — fashion, beauty, pharmacy (OHGA), and a supermarket open 7 AM–11 PM for snacks, toiletries, and anything you forgot. Solaria Plaza is right next door with more shops and connects directly to the Tenjin underground mall. Both open 10 AM–8:30 PM daily.

💴 Cash — Always Have Some

Yatai stalls are cash only. Small shrines and some Yufuin snack stalls too. Keep ¥15,000–20,000 cash on you at all times. ATMs at 7-Eleven accept foreign cards.

🌡️ June Weather — It Is Hot

27–32°C, humid, rainy season. Bring a compact umbrella — it will rain at least once. The underground mall connects most of Tenjin without going outside. Light fabrics only.
